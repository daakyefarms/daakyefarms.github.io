Our Heritage & Vision

The Story Behind Daakye Legacy Farms

Built from a passion for organic agriculture, ethical animal welfare, and bringing clean, hygienic pork to Ghanaian homes and restaurants.

Our Origins

Rooted in Nsawam, Driven by Ethical Standards

Daakye Legacy Farms was established with a clear goal: to counter the flood of low-grade, chemical-laden meat by cultivating wholesome livestock right here in Nsawam, Ghana.

We believe that how animals are raised directly impacts how they taste and nourish our bodies. That's why our pigs live in well-ventilated, clean pens and are nourished with pure, organic grain and tuber feed formulated daily on the farm.
