Strict Hygiene & Food Safety

Our Quality & Hygiene Standards

From the feed we mix to the sanitized cutting boards, every detail is engineered for superior taste and peace of mind.

01

100% Organically Formulated Feed

We eliminate commercial growth hormones and mystery premixes. Our hogs thrive on a nutrient-balanced ratio of cassava root, high-grade yellow maize, roasted soybean meal, and essential trace minerals. This produces pork that is naturally sweet, firm, and free from off-flavors.

✓ Clean feed troughs cleaned twice daily | Zero animal by-products
02

Continuous Veterinary Oversight

Our farm in Nsawam operates under the regular supervision of registered veterinary officers. Strict bio-security protocols (including visitor footbaths and quarantined transition pens) ensure that our herd remains healthy without reliance on prophylactic antibiotics.

✓ Full vaccination logs maintained | Regular deworming schedule
03

Sanitary Processing & Packaging

Slaughter and meat dressing occur in clean, sanitized facilities. Carcasses are chilled rapidly to inhibit bacterial proliferation. Cuts are portioned using stainless-steel butchery tools and vacuum-sealed immediately in food-safe bags.

✓ Food-grade vacuum packaging | Cleaned and singed trotters & heads
04

Cold-Chain Farm-to-Doorstep Delivery

Meat never sits at ambient temperatures. Orders are packed into insulated cold boxes with frozen gel packs and dispatched directly to your kitchen door in Accra, Nsawam, Tema, or Koforidua.

✓ Delivery confirmation via WhatsApp | Guaranteed freshness on arrival
